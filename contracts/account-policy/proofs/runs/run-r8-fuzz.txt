=== 1. The widened target `recovery_seq_wide` (proofs/src/fuzz_api.rs::drive_recovery_wide, fuzz_targets/recovery_seq_wide.rs)

The release-7 target `recovery_seq` (drive_recovery_sequence) is kept unchanged and still builds; the wide one is a new
target. Input: 3 config bytes, then up to 64 operations of 4 bytes (kind, signer, argument, extra); max_len 259.

Start state (config):
- recovery delay 1, 2, 3, 7 or 30 days; fallback and floor computed by the ORACLE from literals (3 x delay clamped to
  [7 d, 30 d]; delay + min(delay, 1 d)), never from the contract's helpers;
- 0 to 3 second devices (keys 9, 10, 11), each aged (age row at 0) or young (age row at the start time), per bit;
- bands: one of five presets: P0 Face ID only; P1 small (0.01 NEAR + 1000 usdc units, one budget) Face ID, rest Wait 2 h;
  P2 small Face ID, rest Device; P3 small Wait 1 h, rest Both 2 h; P4 small Device, rest Both 1 h (P2-P4 only with a device);
- the account's setup window open or closed (created now or at 0); backup words key 4 present or absent (first words at
  Face ID). Phone key 1, sealed device list.

Operations (17 kinds), each from one of 9 signers: the current phone, the current backup key, devices 9 / 10 / 11, a
stranger (7), the old phone (1), keys 12 and 13 (which may become devices or the backup key):
  0 start_recovery to key 5, 6, 12, 9, 1 or 13          9  remove_key of 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 4, 1 or 5
  1 approve_recovery, right or wrong key / finish time   10 add_second_device 9..13 (new, or the refill of a recorded one)
  2 refuse_recovery None / Some(false) / Some(true)      11 set_recovery_key 4 (renewal), 12, 13, 5, 9
  3 cancel_recovery                                      12 set_protection(one of the five presets)
  4 finish_recovery                                      13 execute: 11 spend presets (below)
  5 approve(any waiting request)                         14 the FB-3 callback on_second_device_key for any callback the
  6 decline(any waiting request)                            contract scheduled so far, with PromiseResult Successful or
  7 run(any waiting request)                                Failed (testing_env's promise_results; state through "STATE")
  8 cancel(any waiting request)                          15 upgrade(code hash) placement (its run makes the
 16 time step: 1 s, 1 h, 12 h, 1 d, 2 d, 7 d, 30 d,          use_global_contract + upgrade_check batch), or upgrade_check
    delay, fallback, floor, the stop's wait (+0-3 s)        (6 / 7 / 8) at any moment
So: several devices, aged and young, added at any time (also during a recovery and during a freeze: the add of 12/13 runs
when co-signed during a recovery, or at once in the setup window); removals of devices, of the backup key (refused), of
the phone's own key (refused), of fresh keys 12/13 (the RK sequence: remove_key(12), set_recovery_key(12), run the words
first, then the removal must be refused); spends and their co-signing or declining by young and aged devices during a
dispute; the stop; upgrades; the FB-3 callback in all four (result x refill) combinations.

Spend presets (receiver, steps; the oracle counts what they move from literals: Σ deposits + Σ gas x 2e8 as "near"):
 0 bob 0.001 NEAR transfer; 1 bob 5 NEAR transfer; 2 usdc ft_transfer_call 1 unit, 200 Tgas (RS-2's case); 3 usdc
 ft_transfer 500 units, 10 Tgas; 4 bob 2 x storage_deposit at 200 Tgas (400 Tgas: RS-1's case, must be refused); 5 bob
 do_it (unmeasurable: strictest); 6 bob storage_deposit with 0-510 Tgas (fuzzer-chosen); 7 two near_deposit calls with
 0-319 Tgas each; 8 usdc ft_transfer_call 2000 units (over the small band); 9 intents.near mt_transfer of 10 usdc units;
 10 bob 1-10 withdraw_all calls at 30 Tgas each (30-300 Tgas in all).

The oracle (an independent shadow: phones, backup key, devices with the moment each one's age counts from, the recovery,
the device's yes (key, time, device), the one stop, the freeze, the bands, every waiting request with its kind, finish time,
device need, approval and co-signer, and the scheduled callbacks). A refusal (NEAR panic) restores the account and its
cells; a Rust panic that is no refusal is a crash. After EVERY accepted call it checks the call was allowed and then that
the contract agrees with the shadow EXACTLY on: the waiting request ids, the second devices, the backup key, the recovery,
the freeze cell, the bands, the stop-asked cell, the phone list (sealed), the number of devices of age, and
recovery_status (approved, needs_device). The rules checked at the call:
- everything the release-7 oracle checked, for several devices: only the backup key starts / finishes; a finish never before
  the delay, and with any device of age never before the fallback without a device of age's yes to THIS (key, time); only a
  device of age says yes or refuses, only to the exact waiting recovery; a plain refusal ends it and freezes now + floor, a
  confirmed one needs the stop and writes no freeze; with a device of age at most one stop per recovery, waiting exactly
  min(1 d, delay / 2); an unanswered stop never ends a recovery a device of age said yes to and otherwise freezes; with no
  device of age the stop ends at once and freezes; a removal waits >= floor, a device of age's >= max(floor, fallback); after
  a finish the new key is the only phone and young devices are gone; liveness: the words' finish of a due recovery and a
  device of age's yes to the exact waiting recovery succeed;
- RK: no receipt deletes the CURRENT backup key's access key unless it is re-added in the same batch (a renewal), and the
  run of a removal of the key that is now the backup must be refused; likewise for the phone's key and a recorded device's
  key (NEAR's keys follow the records);
- FB-3: a Failed add's callback leaves the device unrecorded (and the bands converted if it was the last); a Failed refill's
  callback never unrecords and adds the key again; a Successful callback changes nothing; no callback deletes any key;
  a refill (add_second_device of a recorded device) goes at once, never removes it, renews the key, schedules the callback;
- a young device's yes never co-signs: a young device approves only what needs a device; its yes leaves no co-sign on
  record (and never replaces an aged one's); whether a request is co-signed is the shadow's own (approved, co-signer still
  recorded and of age NOW);
- nothing the phone asked alone survives a recovery start, a stop, a refusal, new words during a recovery or a finish (the
  exact id list must match the shadow's drop of every request not co-signed), and while a recovery waits only co-signed
  requests or the stop run, by run or by approve (approve runs a request exactly when the shadow says it may);
- frozen accounts never run anything at Face ID: while a recovery waits or the freeze holds, execute never runs at once and
  waits >= floor; outside, it runs at once only when the oracle's own band for the request is Face ID;
- RS-1: execute is accepted only if every step <= 300 Tgas and the saturating sum <= 250 Tgas;
- RS-2 and the bands: the placed level is at least the oracle's band for what the request alone moves (deposits + gas x 2e8
  as NEAR; unmeasurable -> strictest): the wait and the device need of that band (24-hour history only adds);
- settings (protection, add device, remove key, new words, upgrade) wait >= the floor at the strictest level, except the
  setup window's first device / protection and the first backup key; a key never takes a second role (add, new words,
  recovery to a key in use is refused); decline only of what needs a device or, in a dispute, by a device of age, never a
  device's removal; owner methods only from the current phone.

=== 2. The `contract` target (fuzz_targets/contract.rs), release-8 additions
- sub-target 0 (`moves`): besides "near >= Σ deposits", now RS-2: "near" >= Σ deposits + Σ gas x 2e8 (saturating, literal

=== 6. Finding R8-F1 on lib 5a39e768 (from the widened oracle's "the phone's key is never deleted" rule) — FIXED in e951e00a

How it was found, honestly: not by a fuzzing run. While the 5a39e768 campaign ran, reading the oracle's rule against the
RemoveKey finish suggested the path; an 8-call probe written by hand ($WORKDIR/r8/probe/phone_removal) made the
REAL 5a39e768 build report it. The 46-min strict campaign on 5a39e768 had not reached it, and the F1 control in section 7
is not reached by 5 min of fuzzing either: the oracle catches it, the fuzzer's random search does not find the 8-call
sequence within those budgets.


R8-F1 (Informational / Low, liveness; same class as the re-score's RK item: a removal checked only when it is ASKED).
`remove_key` refuses the phone's own key (`public_key != signer_key()`) and the backup key; at RUN time release 8
re-checks only the backup key. A removal of a key that is NOT on the account, co-signed by a second device of age, survives
a recovery start (it is co-signed); if the recovery then moves the account to exactly that key, the removal later runs and
deletes the NEW phone's access key and empties the sealed device list: the account has no phone until the words recover
it again. Minimal sequence (decoded probe $WORKDIR/r8/probe/phone_removal, reported by the real r8 build:
"a removal deleted the key of the current phone (5)"):
  delay 2 d, one second device (9) of age, words 4, bands Face ID only.
  1 phone:   remove_key(K5)           -> waits the floor (3 d); K5 is on no role, so it is accepted
  2 dev 9:   approve(that id)         -> co-signed (aged), not run (its wait is not over)
  3 words:   start_recovery(K5)       -> K5 is not "in use" (key_in_use does not look at waiting removals); the co-signed
                                         removal is kept
  4 dev 9:   approve_recovery(K5, t)  5 +2 d   6 words: finish_recovery -> the phone is K5, device list [K5], sealed
  7 +1 d (the removal's 3 d are over) 8 phone K5: run(removal id)  -> devices.keys = [], delete_key(K5)
  (step 8 can also be the laptop's approve(id), which runs it at once now that no recovery waits.)

Impact: no money moves; it needs the phone AND a second device of age to co-sign the removal of a key that is on no role,
and then a recovery to exactly that key, so in practice it is self-inflicted or needs two of three (which already control
