  (1) "no phone key on NEAR" after [Callback(1, true), Callback(0, false)]: the harness ran the RETRY's receipts and callback
      first (completing the recovery), and only then the FIRST finish's delete of the new key (which deleted the new phone's
      key) and its add (refused). NEAR runs one account's receipts in the order they were made (local receipts at once,
      delayed receipts first in, first out), so the first finish's delete and add always run before the retry's, and its
      callback before the retry's. Fix: the explorer runs only the oldest finish on its way (FIFO).
  (2) "a lost callback changed the contract" after [Callback(1, _), CallbackLost(0)]: the check wrongly required a FINISHING
      record to remain; a lost callback must leave the storage as it was, record or not. Fix: compare the storage.
  Neither is a contract fault. The exhaustive run and the walks above used harness 1675fe17… (no FIFO): a superset of the
  orderings, no counterexample. Two finishes on their way take at least 3 actions after a ready recovery, which depth 4 from
