On the two killed ones: 853 is `on_recovery_key`'s guard on taking an added key off NEAR again when its recovery didn't finish ("unless a role holds it": not a second device, not the backup words, not on the phone list). Through the contract's methods it can't be reached with a key that holds a role: the callback acts only for the finish on record, a finish's new key is a waiting recovery's new key, and from the start of that recovery until the callback `key_in_use` (the waiting recovery's key, then the Finishing record's) refuses that key every role and `device_gate` refuses it as a phone. So the guard is belt and braces, not a bug. The killing test puts the Finishing record in storage by hand (the contract's borsh layout) to drive the guard with each role's key.
